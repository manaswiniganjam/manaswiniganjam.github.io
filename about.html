ABOUT

Hello everyone! I am a PhD student, my research focuses on understanding carbon dynamics, ecosystem services of terrestrial biomes and their spatial characterization using spatial data and carbon modeling. I specialize in using spatial data to understand the patterns and mechanisms of biogeochemical cycling. I strive to combine my knowledge of spatial data science with my expertise in GIS and remote sensing to develop efficient models of environmental change. In addition to my research interests, I am also passionate about the emerging geospatial technologies to monitor environmental resources. I am always looking for innovative ways to use geospatial data to gain insights into climate change, and I enjoy sharing my thoughts on these topics in this blog. I hope to contribute to the ongoing research to develop informed decisions and strategies to combat climate change and disturbance effects on forest resources and promote sustainability. I'm currently working on a project that uses ecosystem process-based modeling to evaluate optimizing carbon sequestration for the southeastern United States. Additionally, I am also trying to find data resources to better understand changes in belowground processes and soil carbon dynamics due to disturbances caused by human activities. On my personal blog, I share my thoughts and ideas around my research work, world of ecology, data science and GIS. Through this blog, I hope to connect with readers, discuss science, sustainability, and life in research and how we can use scientific data and research to create a better future for our planet. I’m really excited about being part of this amazing journey and hope that my work can contribute towards a better understanding of our environment. Thank you for visiting my page!
